Adidas Sales Analysis

A Power BI dashboard designed to bring sales performance, profit, volume, geography, product mix, and retailer contribution into one clear view.

RoleDashboard designer and analyst
ToolsPower BI, DAX, Power Query
FocusRetail sales performance
OutputInteractive dashboard

Overview & objective

A single view of performance across the business.

Overview

The project uses Adidas retail sales data to build a compact reporting view for understanding overall performance and comparing the dimensions behind it.

Objective

Make the dataset easier to interpret by combining headline KPIs with trend, geographic, product, and retailer views that support quick comparisons.

What I did

Structured the dashboard around business questions.

01

Prepared the data

Used Power Query as part of the data-preparation workflow and organised the fields needed for analysis.

02

Defined measures

Used DAX-based reporting measures to surface the core KPIs shown in the dashboard.

03

Designed the view

Grouped summary metrics and comparative visuals into a consistent layout that moves from overall performance to specific segments.

Tools used: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, data visualisation, and KPI reporting.

The product comparison places Men’s Street Footwear first at $209M, while the monthly view shows a low of $57M in March before sales rise through the middle of the year. These are descriptive findings visible in the supplied dashboard; they are not presented as causal claims.

What I learned

Good dashboards are selective.

This project strengthened my understanding of how to organise a reporting page around the questions a stakeholder is likely to ask: how the business is performing, where the performance comes from, and which segments are worth investigating next. It also reinforced the importance of consistent labels, comparable scales, and separating what the data shows from assumptions about why it happened.
